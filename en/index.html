ProjectsWorks

A few things I've made.

  1. AIOJ2026An online judge for competitive-programming students and coaches. Beyond solving and judging, we want to record how students practise, like how a solution got fixed step by step, and organise it for AI to use. AI already explains problems well; what it lacks is knowing the student.
  2. OnePKU2026A small PKU campus tool. It started as an attempt at a PKU version of oneTHU; PKU's sites turned out not to work the same way, so I picked the few features I use: lecture replays, course files, assignments and grades.
  3. My Life2026An app I made to help my grandpa record his life. You don't need to write or know how to use AI. Open it and talk, and an agent slowly sorts what you say into a timeline, people and places, all kept on your own phone.
